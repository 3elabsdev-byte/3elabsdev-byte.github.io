Minio · Support

Minio: Baby Tracker

A question, an idea or a bug to report? E-mail us at 3elabs.dev@gmail.com. The easiest way is from inside the app: Settings → Contact us. Mentioning the app version and your phone model helps us help you faster.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my records kept?

Only on your phone. You don’t need an account, and your records are never sent to us.

How do I move my records to a new phone?

On the old phone, create an encrypted backup file with Settings → Create a backup. On the new phone, install Minio, tap Settings → Restore from backup and enter the backup’s password.

How do I restore Minio Pro on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, then tap Settings → Restore purchases in Minio.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Minio. On Android: Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. After you cancel, Pro stays on until the end of the paid period.

Which devices does the AI work on?

iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, the iPhone 16 family and newer iPhones, with iOS 26 or later and Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri. The AI runs on your phone; it needs no internet and nothing is sent anywhere. On other devices the assistant shows the guide’s information.

How do I make reminders ring like an alarm?

Turn on Settings → Reminders ring as alarms and allow it. When you add a medicine or drops reminder, leave “Also ring as an alarm” on.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete all data. Your records are only on your phone, so they are deleted at once.
