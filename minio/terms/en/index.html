Terms of Use

Last updated: 7 November 2026

1. Scope

These terms govern the use of Minio, developed by Egemen Enis Ertaş. You need to accept them to use the app; if you do not, the app records nothing about you and cannot be used.

2. What Minio is for

Minio helps you keep your baby’s feeding, sleep, diaper, growth and health records, set reminders and read general care information for your baby’s age. It is meant for parents and caregivers.

3. Medical notice

The information in the app is general information based on the public guidance of bodies such as the World Health Organization, UNICEF, the American Academy of Pediatrics, the NHS and the Turkish Ministry of Health. Minio does not diagnose, does not recommend treatment and does not replace your doctor.

Talk to your doctor about any concern for your baby’s health. In an emergency, call your local emergency number straight away.

4. AI

On supported iPhones, the AI assistant and the AI analysis are produced by Apple’s on-device model; your questions and records never leave your phone.

AI answers are based on the guide and the records you enter, but they can be incomplete or wrong. Do not use them for diagnosis, treatment or medicine decisions.

5. Your records

Minio does not ask you to create an account. Your records are stored only on your phone; they are not sent to us, we cannot see them and we cannot restore them if they are lost. Use the app’s encrypted backup to protect them; if you forget the backup password, the backup cannot be opened.

6. Reminders

Reminders and alarms depend on your phone’s notification, silent mode and battery settings. Do not rely on the app alone for an important medicine or care task.

Minio does not record or suggest medicine doses; give medicines only as your doctor or pharmacist tells you.

7. Free version and Minio Pro

Tracking, reminders, the free parts of the guide, emergency and safety information and 5 AI answers a day are free. Safety content is never paid.

Minio Pro unlocks extra features such as the unlimited AI assistant, the AI analysis, the full guide, history and charts, more than one baby, the doctor file, memories and the sound library. The subscription is bought through the App Store or Google Play and managed in your store account.
