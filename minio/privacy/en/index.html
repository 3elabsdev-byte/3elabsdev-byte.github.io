Privacy Notice (KVKK)

Last updated: 7 November 2026

In short

  • Your and your baby’s records are stored only on your phone; they are not sent to us and we cannot see them.
  • Minio has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no cookies.
  • The AI features run on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • We only receive your e-mail address and message if you write to us.

1. Data controller

Egemen Enis Ertaş (the developer of Minio). Contact: 3elabs.dev@gmail.com

2. What stays on your phone

Your baby’s name, date of birth, optional sex and birth measurements; feeding, sleep, diaper, growth and health records (temperature, medicines given, symptoms); reminders, photos you add, your parent questionnaire answers and your settings are stored only on your phone, in the app’s protected storage.

The names and phone numbers of the people you add for the shift handover are also kept only on your phone. You send the summary yourself through WhatsApp or Messages; Minio never sends it on its own, and we do not see what is sent.

This information is processed on your phone only so that the app can work for you; it is not transferred to us or to anyone else. Health records are special-category data; we have no access to them.

An encrypted backup file you create stays under your control. If your phone’s iCloud or Google backup is on, the operating system may include app data in the backup in your own account, under Apple’s or Google’s terms.

3. What reaches us

If you e-mail us for support, your e-mail address and message are processed only to answer your request and until it is resolved (KVKK Art. 5/2-c and f). Because e-mails are received through Gmail, they are stored on Google’s servers abroad; this transfer is incidental under KVKK Art. 9/6.

Minio Pro payments are taken by Apple or Google; we have no access to your payment details. The app checks your subscription status with the store on your phone only.

4. Permissions

Notifications are used for reminders; the camera and photos only when you choose to add a photo; Face ID or fingerprint for the app lock (your phone does the check, no biometric data reaches us); and the alarm permission only when you choose to set a reminder as an alarm. When you choose a person from your contacts, your phone’s own picker opens; Minio does not access your contacts and receives only the number you choose. You can turn any permission off in your phone’s settings at any time.

5. Your rights

Under KVKK Art. 11 you have the right to learn whether your personal data are processed, to request information, to learn the purpose of processing and the recipients of transfers, to ask for correction or deletion, to object and to claim compensation for damage.

Since your records are on your phone, you can view, correct or delete them in the app at any time (Settings → Delete all data). You can send requests to 3elabs.dev@gmail.com; they are answered free of charge within 30 days at the latest. Your right to complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board is reserved.

6. Changes

When this notice is updated, the new version is shown in the app.
