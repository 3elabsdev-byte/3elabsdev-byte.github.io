Minio

Baby tracker: feeding, sleep, diapers and health

Minio keeps your baby’s feeding, sleep, diaper and health records with a single tap. No account needed; your records stay on your phone.

  • One-tap logging for breastfeeding, bottles, diapers and sleep
  • A guide matched to your baby’s week
  • Medicine, drops and care reminders, optionally as alarms
  • Shift handover: a summary of the last hours by WhatsApp or SMS in one tap
  • An AI assistant that runs on supported iPhones, on the device
